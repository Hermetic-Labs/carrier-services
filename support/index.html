01

Account and access

For a locked account, missing role, wrong organization, unassigned route, or device-admission issue, contact your organization’s Courier administrator first. Administrators control workforce membership and operational access.

If the organization administrator cannot resolve the issue, email FrontDesk@7HermeticLabs.com with your organization name, device platform, and a short description. Never send a password, access token, patient name, barcode value, or delivery photo.

02

Active route or delivery issue

Follow your organization’s live dispatch and safety procedure for a time-sensitive pickup, delivery, chain-of-custody concern, or emergency. This support channel does not replace dispatch or emergency services.

For a non-urgent application issue, capture the time, screen name, and visible error reference. Keep the work queued on the device when Courier reports an offline state; do not repeat a custody action unless the app explicitly asks you to.

03

Device permissions and sync

Camera, location, and microphone access can be reviewed in the device’s system settings. A disabled permission affects only the workflow that needs it. Reopen Courier after changing a permission.

If work remains queued after connectivity returns, use the in-app Sync action once and keep the app open briefly. Record the displayed sync state and cursor before escalating; do not uninstall the app or clear local data while pending custody work remains.

04

Privacy and data requests

Ask your organization administrator to access, correct, export, or delete workspace data. You may also email FrontDesk@7HermeticLabs.com with the subject “Courier Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the organization responsible for the workspace.

Do not include patient details, route contents, custody evidence, credentials, or access tokens in the request. Read the full Courier Privacy Policy.

05

Security reports

Report a suspected account compromise, exposed credential, cross-organization data issue, or security defect promptly to FrontDesk@7HermeticLabs.com. Describe the impact and safe reproduction steps, but do not include live secrets or unnecessary sensitive data.
