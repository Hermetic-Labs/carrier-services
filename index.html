Hermetic operating standard

Technology serves the handoff. It does not replace responsibility.

The operating model is being designed so a route, scan, provider response, or automated suggestion never silently becomes proof of completion. Consequential actions remain attributable to authorized people and systems.

  1. Define

    Write the service boundary, handling requirements, and acceptance criteria before dispatch.

  2. Observe

    Keep authoritative route, custody, and exception states visible without inventing progress.

  3. Confirm

    Require the right evidence and human authority before representing work as complete.
