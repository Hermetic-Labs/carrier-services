01

Scope and roles

Courier is an authorized workforce and operations platform provided by Hermetic Labs LLC. It supports dispatchers, drivers, receiving staff, and administrators; it is not designed as a consumer patient portal.

An organization using Courier generally determines the jobs, people, integrations, and retention rules in its workspace. Depending on the deployment and contract, that organization may be the controller or business responsible for the data, while Hermetic Labs processes data to provide the service.

02

Data the service processes

Courier may process account and identity data such as name, work email, organization membership, role, Entra user identifier, and admitted-device identifier.

Operational data may include jobs, routes, stops, addresses, package or manifest identifiers, barcode values, custody actions, timestamps, exception notes, witness names, signatures when enabled, sync state, command results, and provider event history.

With the appropriate device permission and an active workflow, Courier may process precise location, photographs or videos used as protected delivery evidence, and microphone audio used for an operator-initiated model interaction. Support communications and limited diagnostics may also be processed when needed to secure or operate the service.

03

Why data is used

Data is used to authenticate authorized users, assign and complete work, verify package identity and chain of custody, synchronize offline work, route and recover operations, answer authorized questions about the current workspace, generate requested operational drafts, investigate failures, prevent misuse, and maintain an auditable record.

Courier does not use personal data for third-party advertising, does not sell personal data, and does not track people across unrelated companies’ apps or websites.

04

Device permissions and offline storage

Camera, location, photo, and microphone access are requested only when a feature requires them and remain subject to device controls. Denying a permission may limit that feature without changing unrelated access.

The driver app is designed to keep assigned route state and pending custody commands in an encrypted local database so work can continue during a network interruption. The outbox synchronizes to the organization’s cloud service after connectivity returns. Authentication tokens are not intended to be stored in web local storage or the operational database.

05

Cloud, AI, and connected providers

Courier uses Hermetic Labs-managed Microsoft Azure services for identity, APIs, data, files, mapping, messaging, and real-time updates. When enabled by an organization, a model service may receive the minimum workspace context needed to answer an authorized request or produce a requested draft.

Configured delivery providers—such as Onfleet, Bringg, or Detrack—may receive or return the task and event data needed for that integration. Each provider processes data under its own terms and the organization’s configuration. Courier does not silently enable an optional connector.

06

Disclosure and protection

Data may be disclosed to the user’s organization, its authorized personnel, configured service providers, and infrastructure processors; when required to comply with law or protect users and the service; or as part of a properly governed business transaction.

Courier uses access controls, tenant boundaries, encrypted transport, protected storage, short-lived credentials, and auditable commands. No security measure can eliminate every risk, so suspected exposure should be reported promptly without emailing access tokens, patient details, or delivery evidence.

07

Retention, access, and deletion

Operational records are retained according to the organization’s configuration, customer agreement, legitimate audit and security needs, and applicable legal obligations. Local offline data is removed or replaced as work is synchronized, expired, or the admitted device is reset. Some immutable audit entries may need to remain even when related profile data is corrected or deleted.

Users should first contact their organization administrator to access, correct, export, or delete workspace data. Privacy requests may also be submitted through the Courier support page. Hermetic Labs may need to verify identity and route the request to the responsible organization.

08

Sensitive data and minors

Courier is built to minimize sensitive narrative data and is not intended to hold a longitudinal clinical chart, diagnosis narrative, or medication history. Operational content can still contain health-related or otherwise sensitive information, so organizations must configure access and integrations accordingly.

The service is for authorized workforce users and is not directed to children.

09

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Courier features, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised date and, when appropriate, communicated through the service or the customer relationship.

Privacy questions can be sent to FrontDesk@7HermeticLabs.com with the subject “Courier Privacy Request,” or by phone at +1 (678) 800-8072. Do not include patient details, credentials, or custody evidence in ordinary email.
